Peran desain sistem identitas

Kite & Field · Bandung · Hybrid

Browse sample roles →Prepare a conversation

Susun identitas yang mudah digunakan.

Lead the transition from a visual direction to a practical identity system for a small multidisciplinary studio. The sample role brings together design judgement, clear documentation and thoughtful critique.

Outcomes

Create a core set of layouts, type rules and asset conventions; trial them on a campaign and a service document before documenting the system.

Evidence to discuss

Bring a project where a system had to support different content lengths or channels. Explain the constraints, the rules you removed, and how another person learned to use the result.

Working agreement

This fictional hybrid role is based in Bandung. Agree the studio days, decision owner, review cadence and production support before considering an offer.

LevelLead
CompensationNot specified in this sample

Ask about range and benefits before investing time in an interview. This page does not accept applications.
